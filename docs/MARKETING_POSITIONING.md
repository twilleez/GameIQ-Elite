# GameIQ Elite — Marketing Positioning

## Working headline
**Your basketball sideline command center.**

## Product story
### Track
Capture live player stats, score, game clock, shot locations and game context from the sideline.

### Understand
Turn recorded data into player comparisons, game reports, efficiency views, coaching insights and season trends.

### Improve
Use saved games and verified analytics to identify repeat patterns and guide player/team development decisions.

## Initial target customer
Individual basketball coaches, AAU teams and high-school programs that want a faster alternative to manual stat sheets and disconnected spreadsheets.

## Claims we can currently support
- Live basketball stat tracking.
- Shot charting with automatic 2PT/3PT classification.
- Game clock and quarter/OT tracking.
- Player and team statistics.
- Player comparisons.
- Saved-game/season history.
- CSV/JSON/report export.
- Offline-first PWA behavior.

## Claims that remain gated
Do not advertise these as production-ready until PM signs them off:
- Hosted AI Coach for paying customers.
- Paid Pro/Team subscriptions.
- Multi-coach collaboration.
- Parent sharing portal.
- League administration dashboard.
- Custom organization branding.
- Cloud synchronization of full game/team data.

## Conversion sequence
1. Visitor sees the courtside workflow, not a generic AI promise.
2. Free user adds a roster and records a real game.
3. Saved game produces useful analysis/reporting.
4. Pro upsell is tied to verified advanced value such as hosted AI and expanded history/analytics.
5. Program/Team upsell begins only after collaboration features exist and are PM-tested.

## Guardrail
Marketing must not claim increased wins, recruiting outcomes, injury prevention, player improvement percentages or other outcomes unless GameIQ has credible evidence supporting the specific claim.
